# Financial city registry review

Reviewed September 26, 2026. `src/lib/l1/financial-city.ts` contains nine selected product/protocol buildings (eight live products, one live pilot) and six contextual financial sectors. Named products were checked against their issuer, operator or official developer documentation. This is an editorial registry, not an automatically refreshed institution inventory.

| Building | Selected connection | Primary-source finding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Circle USDC | Ethereum | [Circle’s current native-network list](https://www.circle.com/multi-chain-usdc) includes Ethereum, Base and Arbitrum. Native and bridged USDC are distinct. |
| Tether USD₮ | Ethereum | [Tether’s integration guide](https://tether.to/en/supported-protocols/) lists the supported Ethereum USD₮ contract. Other supported chains are not attributed to Ethereum. |
| Uniswap | Base | [Official v3 deployment directory](https://developers.uniswap.org/docs/protocols/v3/deployments) lists Base, Ethereum and Arbitrum among many networks. The registry scopes the evidence to v3 rather than inferring every version’s availability. |
| Aave | Ethereum | [Official deployment list](https://aave.com/help/aave-101/accessing-aave) includes Ethereum Core, Prime and EtherFi markets plus Base and Arbitrum deployments. |
| BlackRock BUIDL | Ethereum | [BlackRock’s March 20, 2024 release hosted by its tokenization partner Securitize](https://investors.securitize.io/news/news-details/2024/BlackRock-Launches-Its-First-Tokenized-Fund-BUIDL-on-the-Ethereum-Network-03-20-2024/default.aspx) identifies the fund, Ethereum launch and investor restrictions. [The November 13, 2024 issuer release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/blackrock-launches-new-buidl-share-classes-across-multiple-blockchains-to-expand-access-and-potential-of-buidl-ecosystem-302304035.html) documents later multichain expansion. |

[Issuer product page](https://digitalassets.franklintempleton.com/benji/) identifies one BENJI token as one FOBXX share. Other BENJI-platform products must not be merged into FOBXX. |
| JPM Coin / JPMD | Base | [J.P. Morgan’s November 12, 2025 announcement](https://www.jpmorgan.com/payments/newsroom/jpm-coin-usd-deposit-token-institutional-clients) states availability to institutional clients on Base after proof of concept. The page’s indexed primary-source text was available although direct fetching returned HTTP 403. It is distinct from private Kinexys deposit accounts. |
| Visa stablecoin settlement | Ethereum | [Visa’s April 29, 2026 announcement](https://investor.visa.com/news/news-details/2026/Visa-Accelerates-Stablecoin-Momentum-Adding-Five-Blockchains-for-Settlement/) explicitly calls this a live multichain pilot and includes Ethereum and Base. No reported aggregate run rate is assigned to Ethereum. |
| Lido stETH | Ethereum | [Lido’s protocol documentation](https://lido.fi/how-lido-works/lido-staking-protocol) describes Ethereum contracts and stETH representing underlying staked ETH, including rewards and penalties. Receipt tokens must not double-count security stake. |

Display rules: product buildings and roads represent documented connections, not measured product traffic. Live chain telemetry remains separate. Six muted outer buildings represent bank deposits, securities exchanges, bonds/credit, insurance, pensions and real estate; they make no claim that every participant is unconnected and no prediction of eventual Ethereum adoption. Land area, building height and distance must not encode financial market size without an explicit, sourced measurement.

Coverage is selected, not exhaustive. In particular, the map omits custody, oracles, identity, compliance, asset servicing and numerous protocols/products. BlackRock also announced Ethereum tokenized ICS fund share classes in August 2026 ([official release](https://www.blackrock.com/cash/en-ie/press-release-t4)); this is separate from BUIDL and should only become a distinct building when the map expands its product coverage. A claim of the “entire financial ecosystem” would be inaccurate.
